About Me

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft, part of the Turing team where I work on LLM post training for Copilot.

I earned my PhD from the University of Bonn, working under the supervision of Prof. Juergen Gall on Computer Vision and Machine Learning. I completed my Master's degree at the Sharif University of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Shohreh Kasaei, focusing on deep learning for visual recognition. I obtained my undergrad degree in Computer Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology.

During Fall/Winter 2020, I interned at Google Research - Zurich, working with Rodrigo Benenson and Vittorio Ferrari
